WHO WE ARE
A MULTIDISCIPLINARY
DESIGN STUDIO

Studio Matsya is a multidisciplinary art and design studio in Jaipur, founded by architect and artist Satyam Sharma. We create wall murals, paintings and site-specific installations in dialogue with architecture, turning interiors into expressive, story-led spaces. Our art consultancy and direction help clients and architects shape a clear visual language and make thoughtful use of the spaces they inhabit.
